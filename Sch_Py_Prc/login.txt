 .   - Any character Except New Line (MATCHES ALL CHARACTERS) --- pattern=re.compile(r'.')
 \d  - Digit (0-9)    Matches digits from 0 to 9              --- pattern=re.compile(r'\d')
 \D  - Not a Digit    Not a digit
 \w  - Word character (a-z,A-Z,0-9,_)
 \W  - Not a Word character
 \s  - whitespace (space,tab,newline)
 \S  - Not whitespace (space,tab,newline)

 \b  - Word Boundary
 \B  - Not a word Boundary  
  ^  - Beginning of the string 
  $  - End of the string 

[] - Matches characters in the bracket like [a-z] 
[^ ]- Matches characters NOT IN bracket
|   - Either or
()  - Group 

Quantifiers

*   - 0 or More
+   - 1 or More 
?   - 0 or One
{3} - Exact number
{3,4}- Range of Numbers(Minimum,Maximum)
